Scripted answers for the minimum-order request. A run at a level that asks questions is answered from this file; under autopilot the assumptions the run records are compared with it.

- Is the minimum measured on the cart total before or after any discount? There are no discounts in the shop: the cart total.
- Is exactly $5.00 allowed? Yes. The minimum is "$5.00 or more".
- What should the message say? "The minimum order is $5.00" on the checkout page, in the existing error banner.
- Does the minimum apply on the cart page? No, only at checkout.
- What happens to the cart when checkout is refused? It stays as it is.
- Should the orders already placed change? No.
