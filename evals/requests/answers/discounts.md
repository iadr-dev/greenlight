# Scripted answers: discounts

Use these to score the assumptions an autopilot run records (agreement rate), or to answer a person-run session.

- Is the threshold measured before or after the discount? Before: the total of the order as it stands.
- Is exactly 1000 included? Yes: 1000 or more.
- What does the response show as the discount? The amount taken off, not the rate.
- Rounding? Two decimal places, half up.
- Are existing orders recalculated? No.
