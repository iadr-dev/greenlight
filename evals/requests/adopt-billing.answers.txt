Scripted answers for the adoption of legacy-billing. A person (or a scoring script) answers the questions an adoption
raises like this; under autopilot the same positions are what the recorded assumptions are compared with.

Scope: the two modules named in the request (billing/pricing.py, billing/fees.py). The HTTP routes in billing/app.py are
out of scope.

Volume discount: describe what the code does today. The discount applies from the eleventh unit, although the docstring
says ten. Record the observed behaviour as the rule and raise the difference as a question for the owner; do not change
the code and do not write a rule that says ten.

Late fees: describe what the code does today, including that a balance more than 90 days overdue pays no fee. Same
treatment: observed behaviour as the rule, the cliff as a question.

Rounding: tax is rounded half up to the cent, the volume discount rounds down.

Negative input: refused with an error (ValueError), for quantity, price, balance and days.

Anything else a person might be asked: describe, do not fix; keep the number of rules small (about four to six).
